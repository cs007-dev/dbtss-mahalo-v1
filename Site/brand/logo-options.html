Mahalo · Brand identity

Logo directions

Six directions, each drawn from a different part of the Mahalo idea: welcome, the Pacific horizon, hospitality, place, typography and heritage. Every option is shown large, as a full logo on the brand's dark green, at real size in the site header, and at app-icon and browser-tab sizes. Pick one, or mix parts of several.

Exploration sketches only. These use the website's fonts for preview. The final logo will be redrawn with the lettering converted to vector shapes and exported as SVG files in full-colour, single-colour (dark) and reversed (white) versions.
