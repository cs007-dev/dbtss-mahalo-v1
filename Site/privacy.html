Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Discretion is the foundation of everything we do, and it extends to how we handle your information. This policy explains what information the MAHALO website collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Who we are

MAHALO is an exclusive private-protection offering of Dumbarton Security Services LLC (“MAHALO”, “we”, “us”), Florida Class B Security Agency License B1900383. This policy applies to www.mahalosecurity.com and to inquiries made through it.

Information you provide

When you use our contact or consultation forms, you may share your first and last name, email address, telephone number, organization, the type of consultation you are interested in, your preferred method of contact, the services you are interested in and any message you choose to include. You may also share information with us when you call or email us.

Please share only what you are comfortable with. Detailed information about your residences, travel or security arrangements is best discussed in a private conversation rather than through the website.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the servers that host this site keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit, which are used to operate and secure the site. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when pages load.

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange a confidential consultation
  • To plan, provide and manage the services you request
  • To send occasional communications related to your inquiry or our services, which you may opt out of at any time
  • To operate, secure and improve this website
  • To meet our legal, licensing and regulatory obligations

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • The MAHALO and Dumbarton Security Services LLC team members who handle your inquiry or engagement
  • Service providers who support our operations, such as HubSpot, which stores and manages the inquiries submitted through our forms, and the companies that host this website and our email, each under their own privacy and security obligations
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or when necessary to protect the safety, rights or property of our clients, our team or others

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, to provide our services and to meet our legal and licensing obligations, and then delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information and limit access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop sending you communications, by emailing info@mahalosecurity.com or calling 727-400-6259. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we may keep certain information where the law requires it.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
