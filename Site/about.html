About MAHALO

The privilege of living well, thoughtfully protected.

Created for those who carry the most responsibility, and deserve to move through the world with the most ease.

Our story

A private offering, by invitation of trust.

MAHALO is an exclusive private-protection offering of Dumbarton Security Services LLC, created for individuals and families, executives, estate owners and luxury hospitality clients who expect protection to be delivered with the polish of the world’s finest hotels.

Rather than a security service, MAHALO is a way of living: estates that remain sanctuaries, journeys that unfold effortlessly and occasions that stay beautifully private. Protection is always present, and never the point.

Mahalo

/mə-ˈhä-lō/  ·  gratitude, respect, hospitality, trust

Why the name

In Hawaiian, mahalo is more than thank you. It carries a sense of admiration, respect and mutual obligation. It is how we see every relationship: a privilege extended to us, and one we honor through the way we work.
